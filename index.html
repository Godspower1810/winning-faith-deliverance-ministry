About Our Ministry

The Winning Faith Deliverance Ministry is committed to helping people grow in faith, experience the power of prayer and live victorious lives through the word of God.

Our Mission

To preach the gospel, strengthen families, encourage believers and guide people into a deeper relationship with God.

Our Vision

To raise faithful, prayerful and spiritually empowered people who positively impact their homes, communities and the world.

Our Faith

We believe in the power of God’s word, prayer, worship, salvation, healing and deliverance through Jesus Christ.
